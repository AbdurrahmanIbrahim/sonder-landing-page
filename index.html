How it works

not luck.

We studied thousands of the videos that worked and reverse-engineered the pattern underneath — the hook, the tension, the reveal, the ending that earns the save. Sonder picks the proven script, then produces the imagery, animation, and voiceover around it — with B-roll of your actual book stitched through every cut.

  1. 01

    Upload your book.

    Drop in your manuscript and 30 seconds of B-roll of your book — the cover, a flick-through, a shot on your desk. PDF only. Sonder reads the whole thing, not a summary. No prompts. No briefs.

  2. 02

    Sonder finds a winning script.

    We've studied thousands of the videos that actually worked, and reverse-engineered the structure underneath. Sonder applies that proven viral pattern — hook, tension, reveal — to your arguments, your stories, your voice. Not a template. A script built to be watched to the end.

  3. 03

    Sonder produces the video.

    Voiceover, animation, motion graphics, captions — and your book woven through it. We stitch your B-roll into the cut so the thing readers are about to buy is on screen. A full production crew you don't have to hire, brief, or pay $2,500 a month for.

  4. 04

    Share, and go viral.

    Post to TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts in one tap. The readers your book was written for — the ones still scrolling past, not knowing — finally find their way to you.
