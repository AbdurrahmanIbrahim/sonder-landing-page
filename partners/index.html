BookReverb

Real readers · optional reviews

BookReverb puts your advance copy in front of independent readers who choose to read it. You see their progress, and any reviews, in one dashboard.

  • Add your book from its Amazon link and upload your advance copy.
  • Real, independent readers pick it up because it interests them.
  • Reviews are always optional, and there are no review swaps.

Why we recommend it. Videos bring readers to your book page. Reviews help them decide to buy once they get there. Sonder does the first, and BookReverb helps with the second.
