BookReverb

Real readers · optional reviews

BookReverb puts your advance copy in front of independent readers who choose to read it. You see their progress, and any reviews, in one dashboard.

  • Add your book from its Amazon link and upload your advance copy.
  • Real, independent readers pick it up because it interests them.
  • Reviews are always optional, and there are no review swaps.

Why we recommend it. Videos bring readers to your book page. Reviews help them decide to buy once they get there.

Adigy

Amazon ads, run for you

Adigy runs your Amazon ads around the clock. It adjusts bids, budgets, search terms and placements every hour, across all your books and Amazon markets.

  • Connects with your own Amazon login. No need to rebuild your campaigns.
  • Shows your royalties minus ad spend, so you see real profit per book.
  • You stay in control, and any change can be undone.

Why we recommend it. Sonder makes your Amazon video ads. Adigy keeps the campaigns that show them running well.
