You wrote a good book. Almost nobody knows it exists.

None of that is a writing problem. It's an attention problem, and attention has moved to short video.

The new way books get found

Readers find books on TikTok, Reels and Shorts now. The videos selling them don't show the author.

Look at the three videos on the right. No face. No voice you'd recognise. No filming. Each one takes a single idea from a book and turns it into something a stranger stops scrolling for, then points them at the book.

That's the whole method: the ideas are already in your book. You don't need to become a creator. You need to know which ideas to pull out, how to open the video so people stay, and how to post enough of them without it taking over your life. That's what the masterclass teaches.

Your book, a phone or computer, and about 20 minutes a day once you've watched the lessons. There's nothing to buy.

No. Lesson 5 sets up your accounts from scratch, step by step. You'll post the same video to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook, so it reaches readers wherever they are. Most people over 50 are on YouTube and Facebook already.
