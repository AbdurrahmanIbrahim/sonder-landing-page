Questions publishers ask

Good questions. Straight answers.

Do we need to send manuscripts?

No. Your ONIX record is enough to start: description, subjects, reviews and any excerpt. If you want the strongest videos for a lead title, you can add its manuscript, but it's never required.

Is our content used to train AI?

No. Your books and metadata are only used to make your videos. They're never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's, and never shared or sold.

Which ONIX versions do you support?

ONIX 3.0 and 3.1, in reference or short tags, as a single file or a zip. ONIX 2.1 was retired by EDItEUR in 2023, so please export as ONIX 3. You can upload a file or set up an automatic SFTP feed.

What if we don't have ONIX to hand?

Write a short summary of a book, paste its Amazon link, or upload the manuscript. All three work, and you can mix them across your list.

Can our authors use the videos?

Yes. Post them on your imprint's accounts, or hand each author the videos for their title to post on their own.

Can we change the style?

Yes. Your first video uses our defaults so you see results fast. After that you choose the look, the voice, the hook and the captions, per title or for the whole list, and you can edit every script before it's made.

What does it cost?

Your first 3 videos are free, with no card. After that, publisher plans are priced by how many videos you make each month. Once your titles are in, we'll recommend the right one for the size of your list.
